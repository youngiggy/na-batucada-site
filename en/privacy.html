Na Batucada Privacy Policy

Effective November 20, 2026 · Applies to the Na Batucada apps for iPhone and Android

Na Batucada is a metronome and score app for batucada practice. It has no sign-up, no ads and no analytics, and the app's developer runs no server that collects users' personal information. The app has no push notifications, and its App Store privacy details say “Data Not Collected”. This page explains what the app keeps, where it keeps it, and when anything leaves your device.

1. Information kept only on your device

  • Settings, scores and the original files you choose (photos and PDFs), the original text of imported or downloaded scores, your practice times and tempos, and your sharing name are stored in the app's storage on your device.
  • The beat, training and score editing and practice work without an account or a server connection.
  • On iPhone, whether this data is included in device backups follows your iPhone backup settings. On Android, app data is not included in Google account backups. Deleting the app deletes this information too.

2. Microphone

The microphone is used only when you turn on hit feedback or the sensitivity check during practice. Sound is processed on the device only to find when you hit. It is never saved as a recording or sent to any server.

3. Photos and PDFs

Photos and PDFs you add as references are kept on the device as they are. They are not sent to an app server.

4. Connecting a score library (community)

The app does not include any group's score server by default. When you register a score library with a link and an access number:

  • The app sends the access number to the server in the link and receives an access key. The key is kept in the iPhone Keychain, or in storage encrypted with Android Keystore.
  • When you refresh the score list or download files, requests and the access key go to that server.
  • That server is run by the group that operates the score library. Please ask its operator about its logging and retention policies.

5. Checking for new scores

The app has no push notifications. New scores are checked over the connection in section 4 when you tap “Check for new scores” or “Register · Check for new” on the score download screen, and when you open the score library. No identifier or token for delivering notifications is created or sent.

On a device that turned on new-score notifications in a pre-release test build, the first time you open the score library in this version the app asks the connected score server once to delete the leftover notification registration (sending only that installation's random identifier) and removes the leftover notification settings from the device. A registration that cannot be deleted expires on the server 90 days after it was last renewed.

6. Sharing a score as a file

A shared file (JSON) contains the score, the sharing name and time, and the publication label if there is one. It never contains original photos or PDFs, access keys, download history or the score list address. Files saved by the recipient cannot be recalled from the app.

7. Tracking and third parties

The app uses no advertising or analytics tools and does not combine your information with other companies' apps or websites to track you. Apart from the score servers you connect yourself (sections 4 and 5), no information is sent anywhere, and each of those servers is protected by the group that runs it under its own policies.

8. Changing and deleting information

  • You can change your sharing name in the app's settings.
  • You can delete scores and downloaded originals in the library, and the original score text in Settings › Stored data.
  • Deleting the app (or, on Android, clearing its storage in App info) erases everything stored on the device.

9. Children

The app can be used at any age, and it does not separately collect personal information from anyone, including children.

10. Changes and contact

If this policy changes because the app's behavior changes, the new version will be posted on this page with its effective date. For questions, please use the support page.
