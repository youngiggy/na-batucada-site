Na Batucada Support

Na Batucada is a metronome and score app for batucada practice. Accurate beats, score practice by instrument part, and hand-technique and tempo training work without an account.

Contact

For bug reports, feature ideas and privacy questions, the easiest way is the feedback form, which needs no sign-up. The form is in Korean, but English replies are welcome. If you have a GitHub account, you can also post on the contact board. Including your device (iPhone or Android), the app version (Settings › App information › Version) and what happened helps us look into it quickly. If you would like a reply, add an email address or messenger ID in the form's last field (답장 받을 연락처, i.e. "contact for a reply"); without it we cannot answer. Never put a score library's link, access number or access key in the form or on the board.

Frequently asked questions

I can't hear the beat

The click plays even in iPhone silent mode. Check the device volume and the click and accent volumes in Metronome Settings at the top right of the Beat screen. Bluetooth earphones and speakers have a transmission delay, so the click may sound later than the screen. For accurate practice, use the device speaker or a wired output.

How do I connect a score library?

Register it under Download scores on the Scores tab, using the link and access number your group (community) gave you. The app does not include any group's scores by default. Without a connection you can still practice with the built-in example scores and scores you create.

Microphone feedback doesn't match what I played

Microphone feedback is a practice aid, not a precise score. Depending on the instrument, room echo and backing audio, it can miss hits or count extra ones. Try adjusting it with the Hit sensitivity test in the score practice input settings, and if it still doesn't match, contact us with your device and instrument.

Where is my data stored?

Scores, settings and practice times are stored only on your device. See the privacy policy for details.
